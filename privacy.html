Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026 · Effective: 17 August 2026

The short version Moona Care keeps your baby's care records so you and the people you invite can see them. We do not sell your information, we do not show advertisements, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. You can export or delete everything at any time.

1. Who we are

MoonaBaby App, trading as MoonaBaby, of Phon Penh, Cambodia, Phnom Penh, Cambodia, operates the Moona Care app. We decide what information the app collects and why, which makes us the data controller. You can reach us at support@moonababy.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us when you create an account

  • Your email address and a password (the password is never stored in a readable form)
  • The name you choose to display, such as Mom or Dad
  • Your language preference

Information you enter about your child

  • The child's name and date of birth
  • Care records: feeds, sleep, nappy changes, expressed milk and where it is stored
  • Health records: temperature readings, medicines given, weight, height, head measurement
  • Which vaccinations have been given and when
  • NSSF (ប.ស.ស) appointment dates you copy from your card, and whether you attended
  • Appointments and reminders you create

Information created automatically

  • The date and time each record was made, and which family member made it
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, which include an IP address, for security and to keep the service working
What we never collect We do not ask for your location, your contacts, your photos, your phone number, your national identity number, your NSSF membership number, or any payment details. The app contains no advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

3. Why we hold this information

Only to run the app for you: to show your records, to work out when the next feed, vaccination or NSSF window is due, to calculate how long stored milk stays good, and to share those records with the family members you have invited. We do not use your records to build a profile of you, and we do not use them to train artificial intelligence systems.

4. Who can see your records

  • Members of your family group. Anyone who signs in and enters your invite code becomes a member of your family and can see, add and delete every record for your child. You control who receives the code. If you share it with someone, you are giving them access to your child's records. Treat it like a house key.
  • Our hosting provider. The app stores records with Supabase, which hosts them on servers in Southeast Asia (Singapore). Supabase processes the data only to provide hosting to us and is not permitted to use it for anything else. This means your records leave Cambodia and are stored abroad.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not share it with advertisers, employers, insurers, the NSSF, or any government body, unless we are required to by a valid legal order, in which case we will tell you where the law allows us to.

5. Children

Moona Care is for parents and carers, not for children. You must be 18 or older to open an account. The records are about a child, and we treat them with corresponding care: they are visible only to the family group and are never used for advertising or profiling.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your records until you delete them. If you delete your account, we remove your profile immediately. Any family group where you were the last remaining member is deleted along with all of its records. Backup copies are overwritten within 30 days. Some server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security reasons.

7. Your choices

  • See your records. They are all visible in the app.
  • Correct them. Every record can be edited or deleted from within the app.
  • Take a copy. Settings → Copy all records gives you your full history as text.
  • Delete everything. Settings → Delete my account removes your account and your records. You can also email complain@moonababy.com and we will do it within 30 days.
  • Leave a family. Removing yourself stops your access without deleting the other parent's records.

8. Keeping it safe

Traffic between your phone and our servers is encrypted. Passwords are stored only as a hash. Database rules enforce, on the server rather than in the app, that a signed-in person can only read rows belonging to a family they are a member of. No system is completely secure. If a breach happens that puts your information at risk, we will tell affected users and the relevant authorities as quickly as we reasonably can.

9. The law that applies

This policy is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, including the consumer data provisions of the Law on E-Commerce. Cambodia's comprehensive Law on Personal Data Protection is still in draft as of the date above. We have written this policy to meet the standard that law is expected to set, and we will update it when the law is enacted.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will show a notice in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.

11. Contact

Questions, complaints or deletion requests: complain@moonababy.com. We reply within 30 days.
